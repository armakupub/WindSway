title=[B42] Wind Sway
description=[h1]What it does[/h1]
description=
description=Trees, grass and bushes sway with the wind, from light breeze to storm.
description=
description=Vanilla can already animate foliage: the "Wind Sprite Effects" option (Display & Performance) lets grass and bushes move with the weather. Trees stay out of it, and on calm days the wind is so low that even the foliage stands still for hours.
description=
description=Wind Sway is about immersion, bringing some life into an otherwise dead world:
description=
description=[list]
description=[*][b]Trees join in[/b]: crowns sway with the weather, scaled to tree size and tuned separately from the plants.
description=[*][b]Calm days keep a breeze[/b]: two sliders set the sway in still air for plants and trees. Real weather adds on top, storms still hit like storms.
description=[*][b]Render-only[/b]: only the sway animation reads the raised wind. Water, sounds, aiming and everything else stay on the real weather.
description=[*][b]No twitching[/b]: vanilla's random no-cause jitter is removed from trees.
description=[/list]
description=
description=Everything sits under Options → Mods → Wind Sway. No other setup: Wind Sway switches the game's wind path on by itself and draws it with its own batched renderer, so it no longer costs frames.
description=

description=[h1]FAQ[/h1]
description=
description=[b]What does it cost in FPS?[/b]
description=Some. Swaying vegetation is drawn every frame, so it can never be as cheap as a fully static world. Wind Sway batches that work into a handful of draw calls, which keeps the cost well below the vanilla "Wind Sprite Effects" path; on my machine a hit only shows zoomed far out with a screen full of swaying trees (see the note below).
description=
description=[b]Do I need to enable "Wind Sprite Effects" in Display & Performance?[/b]
description=No. While Wind Sway is on it drives the game's wind path itself and ignores that option, the mod's master switch is the only one that matters. Turn Wind Sway off and the game follows your vanilla setting again.
description=

description=[h1]Attribution[/h1]
description=The idea comes from [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3775279084]Wind Tree Sway[/url] by Waizee. Wind Sway is an independent implementation with its own shader and batched renderer, and it fixes the side effects of the original approach: raising the wind through the global climate value also sped up the water animation, shifted the ambient wind sound and added a permanent outdoor aim penalty. Wind Sway raises only the value the vegetation sway reads.
