Wind Sway - Changelog
=====================

0.3.2 - 2026-08-24
  - Fixed: character and zombie shadows could turn into a sheet of changing game tiles.
    Thanks to @Ismællö for the report and the screenshot!

0.3.1 - 2026-08-23
  - Fixed: grass flickered into pixel noise while walking.
    Thanks to @Ismællö for the report!

0.3.0 - 2026-08-23
  - Tree animation retuned: gusts travel through the forest, branches move, leaves flutter in patches, evergreens bounce their branch tiers.
  - Bare trees (winter, burned) sway as bare branches, no leaf animation.
  - Grass, bushes and plants bend instead of shearing; damped beside fences and walls.
  - Sliders are now a baseline: the weather shows as it is above it. Default 0.2, settings reset once.
  - Performance: much less GPU and CPU time; dense forest fully zoomed out runs at the 177 fps cap on my machine (0.2.3: 80-100 fps).
  - Fixed: grass and bushes beside fences bent through them.

0.2.3 - 2026-08-20
  - Fixed: on some systems furniture and plants could turn invisible while staying clickable.
    Thanks to @BiohazardBR and @Soldado Bubba for the reports!
  - Fixed: on macOS trees only had the vanilla sway; the tree renderer now runs on the Mac's OpenGL 2.1 path.
    Note for macOS: play at the display's native resolution. Scaled fullscreen modes make the game's sprites shimmer in motion, and swaying makes that more visible.

0.2.2 - 2026-08-18
  - Fixed: objects with their own draw code, mannequins for example, flickered or vanished while fading.
    Thanks to @KΞRΞS for the report!

0.2.1 - 2026-08-18
  - Fixed: objects in unseen rooms stayed lit with room fade (configRoomFade).
    Thanks to @wastrel for the report and the screenshots!
  - Fixed: seam at the base of big conifers; grass in front of the trunk is no longer cut off in a straight line.
  - Fixed: zoomed far out over dense forest, the tree renderer could switch itself off until restart.

0.2.0 - 2026-08-17
  - New tree animation: crowns bend, leaves flutter, wind has direction and gusts.
  - Bushes and all plants sway, no wind thresholds.
  - Trees drawn in one batch instead of one draw per tree.
  - Fixed: jiggle when the wind direction changed.
  - Fixed: plants behind see-through trees could vanish.

0.1.2 - 2026-08-15
  - Fixed: thin edges of indoor furniture showed through exterior walls.
    Thanks to @Ismællö for the report and the recordings!

0.1.1 - 2026-08-14
  - Fixed: containers and other objects could not be clicked.

0.1.0 - 2026-08-13
  - Initial release.
