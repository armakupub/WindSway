Wind Sway - Changelog
=====================

0.5.0 - 2026-09-05
  - The wind ambience rides the gusts: a front that sweeps the screen is heard, and the lull after it.
  - New option "Wind sound level", default 0.5: how much of the mod's wind the ambience hears. At the default the wind bed stays near vanilla and the wildlife stays audible; raise it if you play Windy or Custom. Weather sounds stay as they are.
  - Fixed: after about an hour in one world the grass could shiver at two to three times its pace; a reload cured it.
  - Thanks to Vargr for the report.

0.4.4 - 2026-09-04
  - Fixed: with Vanilla Vehicles Animated and its transparent windows add-on, vehicle windows turned red or flickered red while Environment Reflections is off. The batch left its depth pages on texture units the vehicle shader reads for reflections; they are unbound now.
  - Internal: an empty tree list restores the tracked GL state like vanilla; the status line is also written when leaving a game.
  - Thanks to gibra for the report and the screenshots.

0.4.3 - 2026-09-03
  - Multiplayer is not supported for now: render glitches were reported on servers that do not show in single player. Reports with console.txt are welcome.
  - Hardened the renderers: a page without a GL texture yet stays with vanilla for that frame instead of drawing the engine's error texture; a recompiled shader gets its uniforms and sampler units looked up again; the tree pass stops on a lost program; the depth atlas copies a map only once its depth data is uploaded.
  - Diagnostics: a status line in console.txt every five minutes (GPU, renderer state, counters) and the console command WindSwayMod.setDebugGlTrace(true), which checks the GL state around every draw of the mod.
  - Thanks to Patzzzz and gibra for the reports.

0.4.2 - 2026-09-03
  - The weather override now yields only to rain, fog and storms. A dry front no longer silences the breeze.
  - Fixed: leaving a game that had the mod enabled only in its save could switch the game's own "Wind Sprite Effects" on for good and keep the renderer running without the mod.
  - Fixed: plants next to a highlighted object (a door with the key in hand, a safehouse) could draw twice or out of place.
  - Fixed: lit signs drew dark at night, blood on walls vanished, roof tiles at chunk edges showed a seam. These draw as in vanilla now.
  - Fixed: in a storm, swaying grass tips could show over a fence.
  - Fixed: a depth map copied before its upload left its plants invisible for the session.
  - Fixed: trees baked into chunk textures stood upright beside leaning live ones; overlay foliage moved out of phase with its branches.
  - Fixed: the wind sound floor applied in the main menu.
  - Fixed: the leaf patch drift could judder after some hours in one world. The clocks restart per world.
  - Performance: no GPU timer queries in normal play; the tree pass calibration runs only with the debug log.
  - Internal: renderer fallbacks restore the GL state, caches are cleared per world.

0.4.1 - 2026-09-01
  - Fixed: the wind options only took effect after a game restart.
  - The wind now defaults to Normal.

0.4.0 - 2026-09-01
  - Trees move by species: crown, branch and leaf traits come from the sprite family; hollies bounce their tiers, dogwood stirs in layers, maples carry a dense crown.
  - Plants move by class and bushes by species: woody bushes bend as stem and crown, ferns bob their fronds, dead crops sway as dry stems, flower spikes nod. Their leaves flutter and flicker in patches like tree leaves; snow-laden flora is damped.
  - Plants see their own wind, driven by the gusts instead of a steady blow: calm air is calm, a front shows as it sweeps through, tips lead, a sheen runs over the meadow.
  - Spring and autumn foliage moves by its thinness: lone bush tufts swing near bare, scattered tree leaves get their flutter.
  - Pick your wind: Calm, Normal, Windy or a custom band; the mod plays its own wandering wind while the game's weather is idle. New options "Weather overrides the wind setup" and "Wind sound", both on by default. The sliders reset once.
  - Fixed: burned trees swayed like living ones.
  - Fixed: trunks and branches painted inside the crown shimmered with the leaf flutter.
  - Fixed: fences and walls could show holes or a grey fringe where swaying grass and trees overlapped them.
  - Fixed: bush crowns could stay half faded after fading near the player.
  - Fixed: tree and plant motion could drift or jitter in long sessions; the clocks run exact now.
  - Performance: less CPU work per frame; the grass positions itself in the vertex shader and captured quads are reused instead of reallocated.
  - Internal: the code base was reorganised and cleaned, no behaviour change.

0.3.4 - 2026-08-27
  - The options hook now runs when the options screen opens, not while the main menu is built.
  - Performance: fences and plain walls are drawn by the mod's batch now. Before, every fence piece split it into many small draws; fenced lots and towns run lighter.
  - Fixed: zoomed out, fence wire and grass could shimmer while moving.
  - New option "Tree detail": High, Medium, Low. Medium and Low drop the fine branch and leaf motion for weaker GPUs; on my card the tree pass costs 34 % and 62 % less.

0.3.3 - 2026-08-25
  - Performance: less CPU work per frame in towns and along fence lines. The depth-map cache now recycles its cells, so it keeps working in dense areas and long sessions.
  - Shaders and masks are prepared in the loading screen instead of stalling the first frame with trees.
  - Fixed: grass and plant sway could turn jittery after about two hours in one session.
  - Fixed: zoomed out, trees could blur or pick up bits of neighbouring sprites.
  - Fixed: the depth-map cache could copy a map before it was uploaded; affected plants kept a wrong outline.
  - Fixed: after a renderer fallback the mod stayed on vanilla until restart; it now re-arms with the next world.
  - Fixed: saving the options with the mod running switched the game's own "Wind Sprite Effects" on for good, so it stayed on without the mod. Your setting is kept now; if it is on and you never set it, switch it off once.

0.3.0 - 2026-08-23
  - Tree animation retuned: gusts travel through the forest, branches move, leaves flutter in patches, evergreens bounce their branch tiers.
  - Bare trees (winter, burned) sway as bare branches, no leaf animation.
  - Grass, bushes and plants bend instead of shearing; damped beside fences and walls.
  - Sliders are now a baseline: the weather shows as it is above it. Default 0.2, settings reset once.
  - Performance: much less GPU and CPU time; dense forest fully zoomed out runs at the 177 fps cap on my machine (0.2.3: 80-100 fps).
  - Fixed: grass and bushes beside fences bent through them.
