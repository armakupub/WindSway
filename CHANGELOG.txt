Wind Sway - Changelog
=====================

0.2.0 - 2026-08-17
  - New tree animation: crowns bend, leaves flutter, wind has direction and gusts.
  - Bushes and all plants sway, no wind thresholds.
  - Trees drawn in one batch instead of one draw per tree.
  - Fixed: jiggle when the wind direction changed.
  - Fixed: plants behind see-through trees could vanish.

0.1.2 - 2026-08-15
  - Fixed: thin edges of indoor furniture showed through exterior walls.
    Thanks to @Ismællö for the report and the recordings!

0.1.1 - 2026-08-14
  - Fixed: containers and other objects could not be clicked.

0.1.0 - 2026-08-13
  - Initial release.
