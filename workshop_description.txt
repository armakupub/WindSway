[h1]What it does[/h1]

Trees, grass and bushes sway with the wind, from light breeze to storm.

I built this for immersion: in vanilla the vegetation stands still most of the time, and the optional "Wind Sprite Effects" only shear sprites between random poses. Wind Sway replaces the animation and the renderer:

[list]
[*][b]Trees move by species[/b]: hollies bounce their tiers, dogwood stirs in layers, maples carry a dense crown. Leaves flutter, scaled to tree size, jumbos included.
[*][b]Wind has a direction and gusts[/b]: crowns lean with the rain, gusts roll through the forest, storms bend crowns over and hold them.
[*][b]Every plant moves by class[/b]: bushes bend as stem and crown by species, ferns bob their fronds, dead crops sway as dry stems, flower spikes nod.
[*][b]Pick your wind[/b]: Calm, Normal, Windy or a custom band. The mod plays its own wandering wind while the game's weather is idle; rain, fog and storms take over. Vanilla = the game's wind only.
[*][b]Wind sound[/b]: the ambience follows the mod's wind. Sound only, gameplay untouched.
[*][b]Tree detail[/b]: High, Medium or Low; lower levels drop the fine branch and leaf motion for weaker GPUs.
[*][b]Render-only[/b]: water, rain and aiming stay on the real weather.
[*][b]Own renderer[/b]: grass and trees drawn in batches instead of one draw call per sprite.
[/list]

Options → Mods → Wind Sway, no other setup: the mod turns the game's wind effects on by itself and ignores the vanilla "Wind Sprite Effects" option while it runs. Turn the mod off and your vanilla setting applies again.

[h1]Multiplayer / dedicated servers[/h1]
Wind Sway is client-side: the server runs none of its code, each player only sees the effect on their own screen. Two things still have to be in place:

[list]
[*][b]On the server[/b]: both mods go into the server's .ini, so joining clients download and enable them (keep your other entries, separate with semicolons; 3619862853 = ZombieBuddy, 3782670683 = Wind Sway):
[code]Mods=ZombieBuddy;WindSway
WorkshopItems=3619862853;3782670683[/code]
[*][b]On every player's PC[/b]: ZombieBuddy's one-time setup from its Workshop page. The server can't do that part for you.
[/list]

[h1]FAQ[/h1]

[b]What does it cost in FPS?[/b]
Some, anything that moves is drawn every frame. Wind Sway batches it and stays well below vanilla's wind option; tree-heavy areas even run faster than vanilla on my machine.

[b]The constant movement or the wind sound is getting on my nerves?[/b]
Set Wind to Calm or Vanilla in Options → Mods → Wind Sway, or untick "Wind sound". The default is Normal.

[b]Why ZombieBuddy, can't this work without it?[/b]
No. The wind effects and the renderer are Java, Lua can't reach them.

[b]Options → Mods → Wind Sway says "Java part did not load"?[/b]
ZombieBuddy isn't installed, or you declined the prompt. Check the install steps on its Workshop page. To undo a decline, edit [noparse]%USERPROFILE%\.zombie_buddy\mod_approvals.json[/noparse]: in the [noparse]"id": "WindSway"[/noparse] block flip [noparse]"decision": false[/noparse] to [noparse]true[/noparse], restart PZ.

[b]Added it to an existing save and nothing changed?[/b]
Subscribing doesn't switch a mod on in saves that already exist. Load Game → select the save → "Choose Mods..." → tick ZombieBuddy and Wind Sway.

[b]Something looks wrong or doesn't work?[/b]
Report it at [url=https://github.com/armakupub/WindSway/issues]github.com/armakupub/WindSway/issues[/url], ideally with screenshots and the console.txt of that session (in your Zomboid folder).
